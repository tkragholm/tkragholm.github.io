epistat-register

crates.io docs.rs source

Calendar-year person-time, interval merging, connected components over shared keys and constant-price deflation for register data.

Install

cargo add epistat-register

Rust 1.85 or newer. Licensed under MIT or Apache-2.0.

What it covers

  • Day counts and civil dates, with the SAS epoch of 1960 beside the Unix epoch, and age under a named rule.
  • Merging of intervals that overlap or lie within a grace period, such as residence spells.
  • Connected components of rows that share a key, for example siblings through either parent.
  • ICD-10 codes, including the Danish register prefix, and their chapters.
  • Deflation to the prices of a reference year.
