Projects

epistat is a set of Rust crates for register-based epidemiology. Most estimators reproduce a function in R, and results are the same whatever the number of threads. The guides show the crates in use, and the source is on GitHub.

  • epistat-survival

    Cox, Fine-Gray, Aalen-Johansen and multi-state methods, with concordance and prediction metrics.

    In R: coxph, cox.zph, clogit, riskRegression, mstatecrates.io docs.rs

  • epistat-matching

    Nearest-neighbour, optimal, full, exact and risk-set matching, with balance and effect estimates.

    In R: MatchItcrates.io docs.rs

  • epistat-register

    Person-time by calendar year, interval merging, linked records, ICD-10 codes and price deflation.

    crates.io docs.rs

  • epistat-inference

    Influence-function, sandwich and bootstrap standard errors, intervals and tests.

    crates.io docs.rs

  • epistat-glm

    Generalised linear models by IRLS, the multinomial logit and the elastic net.

    In R: glm, nnet::multinom, glmnetcrates.io docs.rs

  • epistat-sequences

    Distances between state sequences, k-medoids typologies and their validation.

    crates.io docs.rs

  • Foundations

    Random numbers, quantiles and sums that agree with R, linear algebra, optimisers and test helpers.

    5 crates
